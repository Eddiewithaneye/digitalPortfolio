TouchGrass

We built TouchGrass! An app that gamifies going outside and exploring nature. Every time you identify a plant or animal, you earn points and badges. It's literally showing people how to touch grass, but in a fun way! Can you think of a better way to spend your time after coding for 36 hours at a hackathon?
